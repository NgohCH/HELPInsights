## Challenge and remaining evidence

The old function joined the version while taking the survey lock. The port
checks the version and tombstone after acquiring that lock, so a guarded
deletion that releases the lock first cannot supply a stale version to a
new approval. The test exercises tombstoned rows and guarded deletion
sequentially. Source review covered the concurrent disposal and queue
ordering. The runtime did not exercise concurrent disposal or concurrent
reviewer appointment or basis revocation during queueing. It did check
basis revocation before a receipt retry. Future disposal must keep
tombstone creation and deletion in one transaction under the survey lock.

Disabling the test flag and restarting contains unintended fixture entry
or API access. The additive migration can stay applied. A restored volume
with a previously queued event and a revoked basis needs trusted
reconciliation because a retry correctly fails and cannot confirm the
earlier decision. The retained outbox and basis need a separate retention
decision and linkage review before any delivery or live connector work.
The test did not perform a full backup replay, implement delivery or
approve production activation. Independent cumulative review is owned by
the integration controller.

and holds its 202 at the browser boundary. On the baseline it observed the
submit button disabled and the development token field still enabled. After
the repair it observed all seven approval controls and submit disabled while
