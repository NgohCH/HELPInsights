## Independent review and remaining gates

The integration reviewer examined the allowlist, generated database
summary, workspace and exact-version basis scope, lock ordering, and
idempotency. The reviewer asked for explicit evidence that revocation
denies receipt retries and for combined future disposal verification.
A retry after revocation now has a covered 403 result and no additional
event. After loss of the original receipt, that denial cannot establish
whether a previously approved decision was queued. Trusted reconciliation
is still needed before delivery.

Before a real handoff, run the combined retention fixture described in
[Project Listen](../../docs/PROJECT_LISTEN.md). The outbox and basis
retain opaque version UUIDs without version foreign keys so surveyed
content can be removed. Governance still has to set ledger lifetimes,
cohort identifiers and lawful-basis/revocation policy. The synthetic
basis is a test precondition. It supplies no evidence of participant
consent or a production data-owner approval.

Provider selection, reviewer provisioning, welfare referrals, a local
delivery simulator and eventual reconciliation remain open. Rollback
must preserve the outbox and its audit metadata. The prior runtime
requires a matching migration ledger or a compatible build. The
standalone migration number must be reconciled during integration.
