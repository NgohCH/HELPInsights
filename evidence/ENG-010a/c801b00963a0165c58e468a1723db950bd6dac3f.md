  The runtime role lacks direct `UPDATE` on the timestamp. Before the
  deadline the survey is scheduled, and at the exact deadline it is

  not approve. A foreign workspace actor and a platform operations

  The stale repeatable-read deletion failed with error `40001` after

## Independent review and remaining scope

The integration reviewer examined workspace authorization, body parsing,
existing closure behavior, SQL privileges and disposal locking. The reviewer
confirmed the survey revision makes stale snapshots fail closed and
identified a concurrent change to authority that needed row locks. Approval
now holds both the membership and appointment locks through commit. Negative
tests reproduce the authority ordering and stale snapshot failure.

Scheduled disposal, notices, derivative cleanup, post-disposal restore and
tombstone replay, hold review/release, and production appointment provisioning
remain open. A future disposal role needs constrained row permissions.
Privileged TRUNCATE and trigger bypass fall outside the row guards and must
remain outside that role. The eligible status only reflects deadline and
hold checks. It is no authority to dispose while the remaining gates are open.

Preserve the deadline and hold records through any rollback.
The preceding application expects its exact earlier migration ledger and
writes expiry directly. It needs a compatibility build or a coordinated,
approved restore when rolling back. Dropping the guard independently removes
protection and needs explicit approval.
